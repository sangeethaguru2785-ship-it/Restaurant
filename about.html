Our Story

A Symphony of Flavor, Design & Hospitality

Born from a passion for culinary innovation, Stackly Fine Dining opened its doors on Park Avenue with a simple idea: that a restaurant should feel like the best evening of your year, every time you visit.

Chef Julian Rivera had spent fifteen years in larger kitchens, learning that precision and warmth rarely arrive together. He built Stackly to prove they do. The room seats forty-two guests, the menu is written the morning it is served, and the team is small enough that someone actually remembers your last visit.

Everything we serve starts outside the building — with four family farms within ninety miles, a day-boat fisherman we have used for a decade, and a bread baker whose starter predates the restaurant itself. The rest is technique, patience, and a dining room designed so you can hear the person across the table.

Our Philosophy

Three Ideas Behind Every Plate

If a dish does not serve one of these, it does not make the menu.

The Season Sets The Agenda

We write the menu the morning it is served. Nothing is held over, nothing is forced, and nothing travels further than it needs to.

Source Close, Waste Little

Nine farms, one fisherman, and a bread baker within a hundred miles. Short supply lines mean better produce and a lighter footprint.

Service Without Theatre

We would rather be useful than impressive. No tasting narration at the table unless you want one — just attentive people who read the room.

Milestones

Twelve Years, One Idea

The room has grown carefully rather than quickly. Every milestone below is something we chose to earn rather than announce.

“A dining room should make people forget they are in a restaurant.”

Julian Rivera, Executive Chef & Founder

  1. 2013

    The First Door Opens

    Stackly debuts with twenty-two seats, a six-item menu and a waiting list that starts on day one.

  2. 2016

    Our Own Growers

    We begin working exclusively with farms inside a ninety-mile radius and visit every one of them each season.

  3. 2019

    The Bar Program

    Our bar team builds a zero-proof list that matches the chef's special menu course for course.

  4. 2022

    Two Michelin Stars

    Recognised for the chef's special menu, and for a dining room that still seats forty-two people.

  5. Today

    Still Writing The Menu Each Morning

    Same room, same kitchen, same promise — an evening worth remembering.

The People

A Small Team, Held To A High Standard

Everything that leaves our kitchen passes through the hands of a team of twenty-two. Several of them have been here since the first year; the rest were trained in our kitchen and stayed for the same reason.

  • A pastry program that supplies the whole house, not just the dessert menu.
  • A front-of-house team that works as one unit, with no station you have to explain.
  • A continuing-education budget every team member is expected to use.
