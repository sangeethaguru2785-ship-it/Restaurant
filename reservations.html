The Night Itself

What Actually Happens, Hour By Hour

Nobody is timing your table. This is roughly how a two-and-a-half hour dinner runs, so you know what you are booking into.

  1. 5:30 PM

    Doors And Pre-Dinner Drinks

    Seated within fifteen minutes. A drink before anything, while the kitchen watches the room rather than a ticket rail.

  2. 6:15 PM

    Starters

    Three small plates, shared or not depending entirely on how many of you there are.

  3. 7:00 PM

    Mains

    The long part. Roughly seventy minutes, and we will not rush you out of it.

  4. 8:05 PM

    Cheese And Dessert

    The cheese trolley, then dessert. Both optional, and nobody minds either way.

  5. 9:00 PM

    After-Dinner Drinks At The Bar

    Tables turn over at about half past, but the bar stays open and nobody checks the time.

  6. 9:45 PM

    Last Orders

    Last orders for food at 9:45. The kitchen closes when the room does.

Good To Know

How Booking Works Here

Four things worth knowing before you reserve, so nothing comes as a surprise.

Thirty-Day Window

Tables open exactly 30 days ahead at 10:00 AM Eastern. The 7:00 PM and 9:00 PM slots go first.

Two-Hour Tables

We hold your table for two hours. If you are running late, call us — we will do what we can.

Parties Of Seven Or More

Call the restaurant or use the form above and choose “7+ Guests”. Our events lead will set a menu with you.

Access & Dietary Needs

Step-free entry, accessible restroom, and a kitchen that plans around allergies. Tell us in advance.
