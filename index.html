Our Story

A Symphony of Flavor, Design & Hospitality

Born from a passion for culinary innovation, Stackly Fine Dining opened its doors on Park Avenue with a simple idea: that a restaurant should feel like the best evening of your year, every time you visit. More than a decade later, that idea still guides every plate we send out.

Our kitchen works with small farms, day-boat fishers and heritage producers, letting the season set the agenda. The room was designed to stay conversation-friendly, the lights stay low, and the service stays a step ahead of what you need — never in the way.

The Counter

Eight Seats, One Chef, No Printed Menu

Our most intimate room sits directly beside the pass. The Chef's Table seats eight guests at a single walnut counter, and every course is cooked, plated and explained by the chef who wrote it — never handed over the shoulder by a runner.

Twelve courses unfold across roughly three hours. The menu is decided that morning from what the markets offered, so it will never read the same way twice.

  • Eight seats
  • Twelve courses
  • Pairing optional
