Finding Us

Getting Here

We are mid-block on Park Avenue between 64th and 65th Streets.

By Subway

4/5/6 to 59th Street, then an eight-minute walk south. N/Q/R/W to 5th Avenue is also close.

Parking

Valet from 5:00 PM nightly. The Park Avenue Garage next door validates for 3 hours with dinner.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance on Park Avenue, accessible restroom on the main level, and space for one wheelchair per table.

By Car

Load and unload directly at the door. The side street east of us is quieter if you are dropping someone off.

House Guidelines

Dining Etiquette & House Policies

To preserve a calm, intimate dining atmosphere for all guests, we kindly invite you to review our house policies before your arrival.

Dress Code

Smart Elegant

We welcome blazers, collared shirts, tailored trousers, and elegant evening wear. Athletic attire, shorts, hoodies, beach sandals, and baseball caps are not permitted in our dining salon.

Corkage & Cellar

$75 Per 750ml Bottle

A maximum of two bottles per party is permitted, provided the labels are not currently represented on our cellar list. Service includes proper stemware and sommelier decanting.

Young Guests

Ages 10+ for Dinner Chef's Special

Children are warmly invited for lunch and Sunday brunch service. For evening tasting seatings, we recommend children be at least 10 years of age to comfortably appreciate the multi-course experience.

Photography & Devices

Flash-Free & Silenced Phones

Discreet, non-flash food photography is welcome. We kindly ask that mobile devices remain silent and telephone conversations be conducted in our entry vestibule to respect fellow diners.
